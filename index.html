Why it holds up

  • Tested before trusted. Every capability faces hundreds of hostile questions before it ships; confidently-wrong answers are treated as the one unforgivable failure.

  • It refuses to fabricate. No data, no answer. The refusal names what's missing and offers the nearest question it can answer.

  • Replayable by construction. Answers are recorded computations, so anyone can re-run yesterday's answer and get yesterday's numbers.
